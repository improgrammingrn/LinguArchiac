LinguArchiac is an interactive archive created to preserve endangered languages through stories, recordings, and community voices.

Why LinguArchiac?

A language is much more than words. It carries stories, laughter, and most of all, memories, that shouldn't be erased. LinguArchiac aims to keep stories alive through the voices of those who carry the emotion through the words that have been weaved into a folktale.
